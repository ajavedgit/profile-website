About me

From the help desk to cyber defence.

Workspace photo3:4 · replace later

I help organisations find their weak points before attackers do — and build the governance and controls that keep them secure.

My career began in IT support in London, where I learned to listen, solve problems and take ownership. After my master’s degree at the University of East London, I moved to Riyadh and worked in telecom network design with Alcatel-Lucent, then as a team leader at Nokia, supporting STC engineering projects.

In 2022 I completed an intensive cyber defence programme and moved into security full time. Today I implement ISO 27001 management systems end to end, run vulnerability assessments and penetration tests for web, network and mobile applications, and give back to the community as a bug bounty hunter and content reviewer.
